The Symposium on Biometrics, Recognition and Authentication 2026 (SBRA2026) invites submissions on biometrics and recognition/authentication technologies. Both new and previously presented works are welcome.

## Conference Information

- **Dates:** November 12-13, 2026
- **Venue:** Hotel Village, Kusatsu Onsen, Gunma, Japan
- **Organizer:** Technical Committee on Biometrics, The Institute of Electronics, Information and Communication Engineers (IEICE BioX)

## Topics

- Biometric recognition and authentication using fingerprints, irises, faces, veins, palmprints, ears, gait, gestures, signatures, voice, and multimodal biometrics; related image and signal processing and pattern recognition
- Biometric template selection and update, presentation attack detection, and related technologies
- System design, smart-card implementation, large-scale authentication, databases, biometric information protection, performance evaluation, protocols, benchmarks, and standardization
- Applications including surveillance, access control, entertainment, payment, and personal identification for criminal investigations or disasters
- Other topics broadly related to biometrics, recognition, and authentication

## Presentation Categories

1. New submissions: Oral presentation and poster presentation, minimum 4 pages
2. Previously presented works: Poster presentation only, minimum 1 page

Submissions will not undergo peer review.

## Important Dates

- Presentation registration deadline: October 9, 2026
- Manuscript submission deadline: October 30, 2026
- Conference registration deadline: November 4, 2026

## Submission Procedure

Microsoft CMT will be used to manage presentation registration and manuscript submission for SBRA2026. The SBRA2026 CMT conference site is currently being prepared, and its URL will be posted here once available.

Authors must submit an abstract of approximately 300 Japanese characters or the equivalent by the presentation registration deadline. Only authors who complete presentation registration by that deadline may upload a manuscript or revise their registration details until the manuscript submission deadline. Manuscripts must be submitted as PDF files of 5 MB or less. **All presenters must also complete conference registration.**
